Leadership

The people behind MMSMM

Founder & Spiritual Leader

Rabbi Dvir Even Chen

Rabbi Dvir Even Chen founded MMSMM with a clear mission: to help wounded soldiers rebuild their lives and find a path toward healing, dignity, and renewed purpose. Through dedication and compassion, he leads MMSMM in providing support, resources, and community to those who have sacrificed so much.

Vice President & IDF Reserve Major

Nadav Elbaz

Together with Rabbi Dvir, Vice President and IDF Reserve Major Nadav Elbaz helps lead MMSMM's mission — walking alongside wounded soldiers on their journey toward healing, strength, and renewal, and ensuring every dollar donated goes directly to the soldiers who need it most.
