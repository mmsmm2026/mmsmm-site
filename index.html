From Trauma to Purpose

Healing wounded IDF soldiers, body, mind, and soul.

MMSMM walks alongside wounded soldiers on their journey from pain to purpose — through personalized therapy, restorative retreats, and a community that carries the weight with them.

Support a Soldier's Recovery See Our Work

Three ways we walk alongside them

Healing & Renewal

Therapies and retreats

Personalized therapeutic care combined with restorative retreats, helping soldiers regulate trauma, rebuild resilience, and begin the healing process in a safe, supportive environment.

Daily Support & Stability

Ongoing care

Consistent, hands-on support that accompanies soldiers beyond treatment — offering guidance, connection, and stability through everyday challenges.

Spiritual Strength

Reconnection & hope

A dedicated journey to the Ohel of the Lubavitcher Rebbe in New York, helping soldiers reconnect with faith, purpose, and hope at a deeply transformative place.
