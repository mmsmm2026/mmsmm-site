Our Work

Three ways we walk alongside them

Recovery is not a single event — it's a process. Our programs meet soldiers at every stage: the acute work of healing, the daily reality of staying stable, and the deeper work of finding meaning again.

Healing & Renewal

Therapies and retreats

Personalized therapeutic care combined with restorative retreats, helping soldiers regulate trauma, rebuild resilience, and begin the healing process in a safe, supportive environment.

  • Individual and group therapy tailored to each soldier's recovery stage
  • Multi-day retreat weekends away from daily stressors
  • A structured, judgment-free space to begin processing trauma
Daily Support & Stability

Ongoing care

Recovery doesn't end when a retreat does. We provide consistent, hands-on support that accompanies soldiers beyond treatment — offering guidance, connection, and stability through everyday challenges.

  • Regular check-ins and monthly gatherings
  • A community of peers navigating the same road
  • A stable point of contact for soldiers between formal treatment
The NY Soul Journey

Reconnecting with faith and purpose

A dedicated journey to the Ohel of the Lubavitcher Rebbe in New York, designed to help soldiers reconnect with faith, purpose, and hope at a deeply transformative spiritual place — a turning point for many in their recovery.

  • A guided trip for soldiers ready to take this step
  • Time at the Ohel, alongside community and reflection
  • Hosted, in part, by ambassador families in New York
